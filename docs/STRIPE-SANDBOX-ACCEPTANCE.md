## 5. Confirmed gaps and next implementation

**Missing implementation:** `AiWalletCheckoutService` accepts only test credentials and `livemode=false`, so live purchases are deliberately unavailable. It handles only three Checkout event types. `StripeController` ignores ordinary charge/refund/dispute events; adding the wallet metadata marker still reaches a handler that ignores those types. `AiWalletService` funds/charges but has no refund/dispute reversal path. The super-admin `StripeService.refundCharges` calls Stripe without wallet reconciliation and is not a prepaid-refund solution. Unspent, partially spent, fully spent, held/UNKNOWN operations and partial/full reversals therefore cannot pass wallet correctness acceptance. No external account setting fixes this.

**Missing business decisions:** no executable policy here defines refundable unused credit eligibility, partial/spent-credit refunds, fee treatment, dispute freezes/negative balances or restoration after a win. Confirm the governing policy before designing those transitions. Preserve the approved subscription price, prepaid pack values, markup and existing past_due behavior.

**Single next implementation task:** after owner approval of those financial transitions, implement signature-verified, idempotent test-mode wallet refund/dispute reconciliation tied to persisted payment/top-up identities, with concurrent/replayed/out-of-order and spent/held-balance regression coverage. Keep the live-account rejection until that repair and separate sandbox acceptance pass. Removing the guard is not the next task.

External permissions still needed: approve the specific sandbox account and app environment, authorized credential operator, sandbox-only configuration changes, exact allowed test transactions (including the temporary $1 authorization/cancel), clock advances, webhook resends, card changes/cancellation, read-only synthetic evidence collection and cleanup. A later refund/dispute exercise, paid AI calls, production activation, migration, merge or deployment each require their own applicable authorization. None were granted or executed here.
