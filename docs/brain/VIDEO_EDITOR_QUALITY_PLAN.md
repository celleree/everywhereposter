## Outcome and priority

Deliver a clean, understandable talking-head edit from one source video. The largest quality gain is correct take selection and pacing, followed by a clear source-grounded title and readable phrase captions. Safe emphasis crops and conservative voice processing follow only after their evidence and QA contracts exist. Supporting visuals must be useful rather than mandatory. Natural-language revisions must change the requested part without regenerating everything.

Preserve Postiz, FFmpeg, the existing native Cooked adapter, Remotion Player/export, original media, tenant boundaries, financial controls, Standard fallback and explicit review -> save -> composer boundaries. Improve the existing pipeline; do not add an agent orchestration framework, replacement editor, queue platform, or general workflow engine.

## Shared design contracts proposed for parent approval

### C1. Source clock, identity and alignment

1. Source truth is original media hash plus an immutable alignment snapshot hash, engine/model/version/language and known timebase. IDs are stable **within that evidence snapshot**: source word ordinal and source segment ordinal scoped to these hashes. Do not derive identity from text or rename surviving words after a cut. A new alignment creates new identity and invalidates patches targeting the previous snapshot.
2. Keep raw source seconds/samples in evidence, integer half-open source-frame ranges `[in,out)` in compiled cuts, and explicit output-frame ranges on render tracks. Seconds are converted once by trusted code; models may return registered IDs only. Source word timing is not quantized for display. A retained interval maps `outputFrame = cumulativeKeptFrames + sourceFrame - interval.in`; removed positions map to absent. Intervals remain ordered, disjoint, positive and bounded, never duplicated/reordered to manufacture speech.
3. One pure mapping implementation owns forward/inverse interval conversion and whole-word remapping. Audio trims use source sample-clock boundaries corresponding to approved frame ranges. A remap never independently resets streams or drops a declared A/V offset. Initial support retains current known-zero CFR30 clocks; unknown/VFR/shifted clocks are rejected until D1 supplies a verified conversion map.
4. Every retained word must fit wholly within one kept source interval. Do not split a word across an edit. Caption text order/coverage equals retained source word IDs exactly once; overlays, title anchors, emphasis and supporting visual output anchors are resolved **after** take selection. If an anchor is removed, drop the optional dependent cue and report it; do not silently attach it to another word. Required user-requested cues cause a needs-adjustment result.
5. Adequacy receipt separates `mechanicallyValid`, `speechCoverageChecked`, `semanticCutsEligible` and `humanVerified`. Require positive word durations, no unexplained word overlaps, segment/word agreement, source bounds, speech-gap checks and engine provenance. VAD is a veto/evidence signal, not transcript truth. Low-confidence, missing or normalized speech, uncertain boundaries and unsupported language prohibit automatic semantic cuts. Preserve all words and produce review suggestions rather than pretending a transcript is complete. Human-corrected words require timing realignment; text changes alone cannot establish time.
6. Corpus evaluation only: sample at least 20 annotated boundary words per owned fixture (or all if fewer), all within 80ms of audible truth; require 100% annotated hook/claim/negation/number retention. This sample can evaluate an engine/version; it never marks an arbitrary upload semantically eligible. A model/aligner upgrade, weights/license change, new dependency or paid transcription retains its actual approval gate. No mandatory paid realignment in A.
7. Per-job eligibility: the worker-side validation stage in cooked-source-selection.py produces a bounded, hashed seam/replacement receipt for this source/alignment/selection identity. Every proposed join/removal boundary must cite source frame/sample positions, neighboring stable word IDs and positive intervals, >=220ms protected speech/breath padding, global and local-gain VAD/quiet-gap evidence, and coverage/uncertainty findings. Every retained replacement must cite its complete segment and word-ID range, source-clock extent, aligned coverage and the trusted completeness/meaning evidence. Evidence must come from the current native measurements plus a source-bound human verification record or an explicitly validated aligner/coverage producer with current per-job checks; a director explanation or ASR probability alone is not a completeness attestation. No such fully adequate producer has been established for arbitrary uploads in this planning task. Missing, stale, conflicting or unsupported evidence at any proposed seam/replacement keeps that upload in preserve-and-suggest mode. A syntactically valid transcript and a passing corpus do not override this rule.
8. A1 identity/rounding contract: alignmentHash is SHA-256 of UTF-8 JSON.stringify of the ordered tuple ['ep-alignment-v1',sourceSha256,rawAlignmentBytesSha256,engineName,engineVersion,modelArtifactSha256,language,canonicalOptionsSha256,[fpsNumerator,fpsDenominator,audioSampleRate,'known-zero']]. Hashes are lowercase hex; options digest is supplied from canonical sorted-key engine options, preserving array order; the helper hashes no files and cannot attest provenance. Raw alignment digest binds the exact immutable upstream bytes, not retimed words or a normalized text reconstruction. Initial fps tuple is [30,1]; sample rate is the observed positive integer source audio rate. A word ID is the 64-hex SHA-256 of JSON.stringify(['ep-source-word-v1',sourceSha256,alignmentHash,wordOrdinal]). Missing identity fields fail closed. For nonnegative finite seconds, coveringFrames=[floor(start*30),ceil(end*30)); removableInteriorFrames=[ceil(start*30),floor(end*30)). Apply floor/ceil to the JavaScript binary64 product exactly, no epsilon or nearest-frame snapping; reject empty/out-of-bounds results. This may conservatively retain an extra boundary frame. Input word seconds remain unchanged; only explicit frame-range derivation rounds. The caller still needs whole-word containment/per-job seam validation before removal.

### C2. Selection and pacing

Use a two-step plan: bounded candidate proposals, then deterministic compilation/validation. The director proposes source segment/word IDs, reason, duplicate-group identity, replacement/best-complete-take identity and evidence for why content is redundant. It cannot submit arbitrary timestamps, rewrite narration, reorder script, or approve itself. Initially accept only fully aligned complete-take duplicates or explicit self-correction/false-start spans with a retained complete replacement in the same local script position; ambiguous paraphrases remain.

Preserve the initial hook, unique facts/examples, qualifiers, negations, numbers, conclusion, meaningful breaths, dramatic/emphatic pauses and sentence rhythm. No blanket filler, breath or silence deletion. A phrase beginning with “not”, a deliberate repeated rhetorical line, and two takes with different numbers are required negative fixtures. Keep source order. Begin with existing relaxed/Natural settings in video-edit-decision.ts: -35dB detector threshold, 1100ms minimum silence, 220ms speech padding; native quiet-block preparation separately uses the stricter min(-45dB, preset threshold) and VAD veto. Remove only surplus **verified quiet interiors** outside protected speech/pause spans and preserve at least that 220ms padding. A hard length-reduction target is forbidden.

Every accepted cut records exact source IDs/frame interval, reason/evidence, selector version, protected spans and inverse restore operation. Proposed maximum 20 semantic removals/source in A; existing 100 kept-range native ceiling remains the stricter bound when applicable. Selection is a reversible draft, never a source mutation. Keep a complete-take review summary and before/after transcript. Rejected selection leaves the last verified draft usable. Do not route semantic cuts through the existing quiet-block-v1 public edit route.

Native handoff transaction (V2 internal path only): current studioSource exports immutable files and closes its native session before direction; do not attempt to mutate those closed snapshots. Add a bounded V2 open/export-base -> select -> export-selected -> close transaction, owned by the app orchestration service and fixed Unix-socket client/worker. V1 studioSource and public quiet-block routes retain their behavior. Export immutable base evidence/audio into a base subdirectory; keep its source-bound native session exclusively retained through the one bounded direction/selection operation, with an absolute <=15-minute transaction deadline (and stricter existing native operation limits), not indefinite keepalive. Reserve admission before spend; if unavailable, return a proven not-admitted result. Do not hold an unbounded paid pipeline behind a busy native worker.

Persist a server-private selection marker before dispatch with organization/media/job/generation, native request/job/revision, source/alignment/base snapshot hashes, selection operation ID, canonical selection hash and absolute deadline. The private POST studio-selection endpoint accepts only bounded typed ID-derived selection plus current evidence/expected revision, never executable/client paths; native runtime independently recomputes ranges and eligibility from its owned evidence. Worker records the operation binding before mutation and publishes a completed selection receipt only after render/retime/source-plan audio audit succeeds. Identical operation IDs recover the same result; changed body/foreign source/stale revision conflicts. Export selected evidence, captions/decisions and audio to a new immutable selection-hash directory, transfer audio with length/hash verification and atomic publication, then atomically publish a complete snapshot marker. Never overwrite base evidence.json/cooked-audio.mp4 with selected bytes. Only complete selected snapshots feed remapped captions/title/visuals and creative rendering.

On response loss, look up the same request/selection marker and recover the exact completed native revision/snapshot without a new model call or repeated mutation. Close only after the complete snapshot is verified, or on deliberate cancellation/failure cleanup. Expired/lost native sessions or uncertain mutations without a verifiable receipt become NEEDS_VERIFICATION/UNKNOWN; preserve the complete base/previous draft and do not silently re-transcribe, reselect or spend again. A rejected selection restores the prior verified draft using existing checkpoint principles. Native namespace retirement and org isolation remain mandatory. Package the new Python helper and all transitive owned runtime modules in the exact-SHA stage-runtime closure before enabling this V2 path; source presence alone is insufficient.

### C3. Versioning, title, captions, emphasis and collisions

Frozen schemaVersion1/easy-graphics-v1 manifests and receipts retain their meaning. New fields use explicit project/manifest schemaVersion2 and `talking-head-v2` profile in the existing package; new evidence uses schemaVersion2. Add compatibility readers/validators before new producers; unknown versions fail closed. Do not rewrite old artifacts on read. Profile acceptance changes in the package, app receipt gate and director must be coordinated serially.

Title: one 3-9-word, at-most-60-character source-grounded summary or supported promise, with source word/segment IDs. No fabricated benefit, statistic or claim. Prefer a direct extract; a paraphrase still needs human creative review. Opening default display 2-4s, bounded by a complete opening phrase. Near-top means a title candidate with top edge in the upper 40% of the design canvas and fully inside the selected usable polygons, outside protected face/head/hands. Title measured font >=1.25x caption font, maximum two lines, opaque/high-opacity readable block background, tested contrast >=4.5:1. If a readable title cannot fit, show needs-framing-adjustment; never cover the face, shrink it below threshold or silently omit a required title.

Captions: deterministic punctuation/phrase/pause grouping from verified words; 1-8 words, max two measured lines, target 0.8-3s phrases. Permit shorter phrases for actual brief speech; split long phrases at semantic boundaries. Each word appears exactly once in transcript order. Do not hallucinate punctuation/words or merge across cuts/long silent gaps. Default sentence-case, one fixed font/background style, no bounce/scale. Highlight only the active aligned word with a restrained color/weight treatment; no highlight during gaps. Measure maximum styled glyph/stroke extents for every state. Text corrections use a separate reviewed operation and require preserved timing adequacy.

Emphasis: distinct typed track, not an unlocked historical ShoulderZoom effect. Anchor to retained concept/phrase; max two crops per minute, 1.03-1.08 scale, 0.8-2s with >=3s separation; no crop over title-opening or supporting insert by default. Use the source-hash-bound stationary rectangle plus protected padding. Check the complete transformed rectangle across easing extrema, not just endpoints. Skip unsafe, uncertain or low-resolution crops and expose the reason. At optional Q2, compare a native 720x1280 delivery raster when input is 720x1280; a crop must retain at least 640x1138 source pixels with <=1.125x delivery enlargement. Smaller sources skip crops. Q1 core does not depend on a raster change or any crop and preserves its established delivery behavior. Wider 1080 source intake waits for D1; no silent material output-default change. Typography stays in existing 1080x1920 design coordinates, scaled by the chosen canvas; reduced delivery does not change text hierarchy. No moving/multiple-speaker inference.

One deterministic layout schedule spans all event start/end boundaries and caption groups: protect selected platform exclusions + face + title first, captions next, optional panels/graphics last. Reserve one stable caption box for its whole phrase using the union of all obstacles during that phrase; highlighting cannot trigger layout jumps. Title and caption may coexist only in nonintersecting readable boxes. Full-screen inserts may cover source picture only when no protected essential action is lost; speech/captions continue, title stays visible or insert is delayed. Full-screen insert and emphasis are mutually exclusive; lower-priority optional cue is skipped rather than stacked. Graphics and panels cannot occupy caption/title/face rectangles, and optional cue density is capped. For mandatory conflicts, request adjustment; never silently erase the request. Check display preview and encoded samples around every boundary, including selected platform intersection extremes.

### C4. Audio intent and proof

Use explicit `preserve` (default), `level`, or `level-and-denoise` processing contract, versioned and bound to source hash, cuts, channel mapping, sample rate, filter graph/parameters, measurements and resulting PCM hash. Supporting video is always muted. No music, voice replacement, speed change, time stretch or word synthesis.

Two independent proof legs are required: (1) original audio -> exact approved source-cut PCM, with source-plan custody/timing/cut-boundary checks; (2) approved processing -> final encoded output, comparing final against the **processed** PCM with current <=1ms lag, >.995 overall and >.97 audible 1s window correlation. Original audio hashes remain unchanged. DSP may change amplitude/timbre, so do not label raw->processed correlation as “unchanged audio”; separately test unchanged duration/sample count, no lost speech, clipping, bounded gain and listening. Retain the existing no-DSP path and native source-plan checks.

Proposed conservative level defaults: measure integrated speech loudness/true peak, target -16 LUFS (mono distribution may use a reviewed target later), gain limited to +/-6dB and true peak <=-1dBTP. If the target would require stronger gain, leave conservative gain and report deviation. Denoising only with an explicit eligible steady-noise measurement and a bounded tested native FFmpeg filter; default off for unknown/quiet/breathy/distorted input. Proposed maximum 6dB reduction, no gating breaths. Silence/noisy clipping failures are needs-adjustment, not an invitation to amplify harder. Voice/naturalness and edit-boundary listening acceptance is mandatory. Extend picture QA too: known title/crop coverage skips only those unchanged-picture samples, and checks expected transformed source geometry; never disable all picture checks for the new profile.

App resolves timing and geometry; reject invented IDs and out-of-bounds playback. B-roll sourceStart is in asset seconds.
