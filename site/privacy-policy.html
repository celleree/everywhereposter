Publish Everywhere

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2, 2026. Publish Everywhere ("Publish Everywhere," "we," "our," or "us") is operated by Arundel Kramer.

Publish Everywhere helps creators, founders, businesses, and teams publish content across multiple platforms from one place. Users can upload content, generate platform-specific captions and descriptions with AI assistance, schedule posts, publish across supported platforms, and review performance in a centralized workflow.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up for Publish Everywhere, we may collect information such as your name, email address, login credentials, and other account details you provide.

Connected Account Information

When you connect third-party platforms such as Google or YouTube, we may collect and process the information necessary to authenticate your account, maintain the connection, and provide the features you choose to use.

Content and User Inputs

We may collect content, captions, descriptions, media, drafts, scheduling data, prompts, brand information, and other materials you upload, create, or submit through the service.

Usage and Technical Information

We may collect basic technical and usage information such as IP address, browser type, device information, session data, pages viewed, feature usage, timestamps, and diagnostic or error information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and manage user accounts
  • authenticate users and maintain access to the service
  • connect and maintain third-party platform integrations
  • enable publishing, scheduling, content generation, and related features
  • generate platform-specific captions, descriptions, and other content variations
  • display and organize connected account data within the app
  • improve product functionality, reliability, and user experience
  • monitor abuse, enforce our terms, and protect the security of the service
  • communicate with users about service-related matters
  • comply with legal obligations

3. Google User Data

If you connect a Google account, Publish Everywhere may access Google user data only as needed to provide the Google-related features you choose to use, such as sign-in, account connection, and supported publishing functionality.

Google user data may be used to:

  • authenticate your identity
  • connect your Google or YouTube account
  • enable publishing or account-related features you request
  • display or process information necessary for those features inside Publish Everywhere

We do not use Google user data for unrelated purposes.

In particular, we do not sell Google user data, and we do not use Google user data for targeted advertising.

4. AI-Assisted Features

Publish Everywhere may use user-provided content and inputs to generate platform-specific captions, descriptions, post variations, and related content outputs.

These AI-assisted features are used to provide the user-facing functionality of the service. We do not claim that all generated outputs are always accurate, complete, or suitable for every purpose, and users remain responsible for reviewing content before publishing.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only:

  • with vendors or service providers that help operate the service
  • when necessary to provide features requested by the user
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request
  • when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of Publish Everywhere, our users, or others
  • in connection with a business transfer, merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

If you disconnect a third-party account or request deletion of your account, we will make reasonable efforts to delete or de-identify applicable information within a reasonable time, subject to legal, security, backup, or fraud-prevention needs.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps designed to protect information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Team and Workspace Features

If Publish Everywhere allows users to invite team members or collaborators, account and workspace-related information may be visible to authorized members of the same workspace or team as needed for the service to function.

9. Third-Party Services

Publish Everywhere connects with third-party services and platforms. Your use of those third-party services is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

10. Children's Privacy

Publish Everywhere is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. International Use

If you access Publish Everywhere from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other jurisdictions where service providers supporting the platform operate.

12. Your Choices

You may:

  • update certain account information
  • disconnect third-party accounts
  • revoke Google account access through your Google account settings
  • contact us to request account deletion or other privacy-related assistance
